# EcomOS Help Video Scripts

**Format:** 2-3 minutes each | 720p | Background music at -20dB

## Video 1: Getting Started (2 min)

**Scene:** Dashboard walkthrough

> Welcome to EcomOS. I'm going to show you how to get your store connected in just 5 minutes.
>
> First, go to dashboard and click "Connect Shopify." You'll see this screen—just click "Authorize." 
> [Show authorization flow]
>
> Next, select your Shopify store and grant permissions. We need access to products, orders, and customers.
>
> Once connected, you'll see your data automatically sync. Your products, orders, and customers all appear here in real-time.
>
> That's it! You're connected. Next, add your product costs so we can calculate true profit.

**CTA:** "Watch our next video: Understanding Profit"

## Video 2: Understanding Profit (2 min)

**Scene:** Analytics dashboard with sample data

> Let's talk about profit. This is what EcomOS is all about.
>
> Revenue is what you see in Shopify. But profit is what matters: Revenue minus cost of goods, minus payment fees, minus ad spend.
>
> Here's an example. This product shows $500 revenue. But the cost was $200, fees were $15, so real profit is $285.
>
> [Point to margin %] Margin shows profit as a percentage of revenue. This product has 57% margin—that's healthy.
>
> You can add costs per product variant. Click here, enter the supplier cost. We'll do all the math automatically.
>
> At the top, you see your 30-day summary: total revenue, total profit, and average margin. Use this to see if your business is healthy.

**CTA:** "Next: Connect Meta Ads"

---

## Video 3: Meta Ads Integration (2 min)

**Scene:** Meta integration screen

> Now let's connect your Meta ads so you can see what's actually working.
>
> Click Integrations, then Connect Meta. You'll authorize your Meta business account—same process as Shopify.
>
> Once connected, you'll see all your campaigns here. Each one shows spend, clicks, and ROAS.
>
> ROAS stands for Return On Ad Spend. A 2.5x ROAS means for every dollar you spend on ads, you make $2.50 in revenue.
>
> EcomOS watches your campaigns 24/7. If one gets a high ROAS, we alert you so you can scale it. If one's underperforming, we tell you to pause it.
>
> You can also create campaigns directly from here, set daily budgets, and pause them instantly.

**CTA:** "Last video: AI Product Copy"

---

## Video 4: AI Product Copy (1.5 min)

**Scene:** Products page with AI section

> Finally, let's generate product copy with AI—saves hours of writing.
>
> Go to Products, pick one you want to optimize. Scroll down to "AI Product Copy."
>
> Click Generate. Our AI reads your product details and creates:
> - A compelling headline
> - A full description
> - Bullet points
> - SEO keywords
>
> [Show generated copy] Review it. If you like it, click Publish to Shopify—it updates your store instantly.
>
> If you want to tweak it, edit here first, then publish. You get 5 generations per day on the free plan.

**CTA:** "You're ready. Go build something great."

## Production Checklist

- [ ] Background: light gray or white
- [ ] Webcam: top right corner, 240p
- [ ] Music: royalty-free from Epidemic Sound or Artlist
- [ ] Font: Montserrat 24px for on-screen text
- [ ] Subtitles: .srt or .vtt included
- [ ] Length: 2 min ± 5 sec
- [ ] Audio levels: speech -18dB, music -25dB, peak -3dB
- [ ] Upload to YouTube unlisted, then embed on app
