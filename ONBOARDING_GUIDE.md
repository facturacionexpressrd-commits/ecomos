# EcomOS User Onboarding Guide

## Welcome to EcomOS 👋

EcomOS helps Shopify store owners understand their true profit on every product and automatically optimize their Meta Ads campaigns.

---

## Getting Started (5 min)

### 1. Sign Up
- Go to https://ecomos-omega.vercel.app
- Click **"Create your account"**
- Enter email + password
- Verify email (check inbox)

### 2. Create Your Organization
- Enter your business name
- You're set as Owner

### 3. Connect Your First Store

#### A. Connect Shopify
1. Dashboard → **Stores** → **Connect Shopify**
2. Shopify login
3. Grant permissions (products, orders, customers, inventory)
4. Redirects back to EcomOS
5. ✅ Store connected!

**What syncs:**
- All products & variants
- All orders & refunds
- All customers
- Live inventory

## Core Features

### 📊 Dashboard
- **Analytics:** Total profit, ROAS, trends
- **Products:** Profit per variant, margins
- **Orders:** Revenue, refunds, fulfillment
- **Customers:** Lifetime value, location

### 💰 Profit Analysis
Every product shows:
- **Revenue:** From Shopify
- **COGS:** Cost of goods
- **Fees:** Payment processing
- **Profit:** Revenue - COGS - Fees - Ad Spend
- **Margin %:** Profit ÷ Revenue

**Add Costs:**
- Manual entry per variant
- Or connect **CJ Dropshipping** for live pricing

### 📈 Meta Ads Integration
1. Dashboard → **Integrations** → **Connect Meta**
2. Select Meta account
3. Give permissions (ads_read, ads_management)
4. ✅ Connected!

**Capabilities:**
- Create campaigns
- Set daily budgets
- Pause/resume campaigns
- View spend & ROAS
- Duplicate campaigns
- Delete campaigns

### 🤖 AI Product Copy
1. Go to **Products**
2. Select a product
3. Scroll to **"AI Product Copy"**
4. Click **"Generate Product Copy"**
5. AI generates: headline, description, bullet points, SEO keywords
6. Review and **"Publish to Shopify"** or edit first

### ⚙️ Automation (Campaign Manager)
When AI agents are active:
- **Auto-create** campaigns for new products
- **Auto-pause** underperforming campaigns (low ROAS)
- **Auto-scale** budgets for top performers
- **Daily monitoring** of performance metrics

- **Chat:** Available in dashboard (Pro only)

### Common Issues

**Q: My products aren't syncing**
- Check Shopify connection (dashboard → stores)
- Try manual refresh
- Check Shopify app logs

**Q: Meta spend isn't showing**
- Verify Meta account has active ads
- Wait 24 hours (data lag)
- Check permissions in Meta settings

**Q: AI product copy failed**
- Check product has title + description
- Verify quota (5 per day free)
- Refresh and retry

## FAQ

**Q: How often does data sync?**  
A: Real-time via webhooks. Full re-sync nightly at 6 AM UTC.

**Q: Can I use multiple stores?**  
A: Yes, unlimited stores per account.

**Q: Do you store credit card info?**  
A: No. We integrate with Stripe for payments.

**Q: How is my data encrypted?**  
A: AES-256 at rest. TLS in transit. GDPR compliant.

**Q: Can I export my data?**  
A: Yes. Dashboard → Export → Select date range.
